UNITY · INTERACTIVE DEMO

Supermarket Simulation

Watch customers shop, queue and pay. Adjust the simulation parameters to explore how checkout capacity and timing affect the queue.

Use Play and Pause inside the simulation. To apply new parameters, use Simulate (Reset) in its settings panel.

The original Unity simulation, running in your browser. Desktop or a larger screen is recommended for the controls.

Parameter guide

Enter all three values in the simulation panel, then press Simulate (Reset) to apply them. Changing the page language keeps the simulation running.

ParameterMeaning in this simulationWhen increased
λ (lambda)Base interval between customers joining the checkout queue, in simulation seconds.Customers join the queue less frequently.
μ (mu)Base payment / service time per customer, in simulation seconds.Each cashier takes longer to serve a customer.
sNumber of cashiers serving customers simultaneously. Use an integer from 1 to 10.More customers can be served at the same time.

Here, λ and μ are time settings, not rates. The model adds random variation to these base times. Use positive numbers.
